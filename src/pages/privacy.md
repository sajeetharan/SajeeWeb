# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This notice covers [www.sajeetharan.dev](https://www.sajeetharan.dev/),
the personal website and blog of Sajeetharan Sinnathurai. For privacy questions,
contact [sajeefx@gmail.com](mailto:sajeefx@gmail.com).

## Information processed when you visit

The site provides public articles, projects, talks, and mentoring information.
It does not currently offer visitor accounts, an active newsletter subscription
form, or an on-site checkout.

GitHub Pages hosts the site, and Cloudflare provides DNS, proxy, and security
services. These providers may process technical information such as your IP
address, requested URLs, browser information, and request times to deliver,
protect, and troubleshoot the site.

- [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
- [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

## Google Analytics and local storage

The site uses Google Analytics to understand visits, page usage, and referral
sources and to improve its content. Analytics may use cookies and process
information about your browser, device, pages visited, interactions, and
approximate location. Reports available to the site owner are not a complete
record of every visit; browser settings and blocking tools can affect collection.

The site also uses browser storage for interface preferences, such as your
selected light or dark theme.

Learn [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and read [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
Google offers a [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
This add-on is not a consent control for other services on the site.

## AdSense verification and future advertising

The site currently publishes a Google AdSense ownership-verification meta tag.
That tag identifies the publisher account; it does not itself load an ad script
or serve advertisements.

If Google advertising is enabled in the future, third parties, including Google,
may place or read cookies in your browser or use web beacons, IP addresses, or
other identifiers to serve, measure, and protect advertising. Google and its
partners may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on visits to this site
and other sites. Depending on the configuration and your choices, ads may be
personalized.

You can manage Google's personalized advertising preferences in
[My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/) and learn about Google's
[advertising technologies and cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
For participating third-party advertising providers, visit
[AboutAds choices](https://optout.aboutads.info/).
Opting out of personalized advertising does not necessarily stop ads or all
cookies and is not a substitute for any consent required by law.

Advertising will require a review of this notice and the applicable consent
configuration before launch. This page does not itself collect consent or
provide a cookie-preference control.

original WordPress archive. Loading those resources can send the provider your

use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

External links, including Topmate booking links, take you to separate services.
Those services handle any account details, messages, bookings, and payments
you provide to them under their own privacy policies.

## Information you send directly

### Ask Sajee contact guide

The Ask Sajee widget is a guided contact menu, not live chat or an AI assistant.
It does not collect or store message text, send messages automatically, or forward
questions to Topmate. Topmate links open its own service pages, where you review
pricing and availability and enter any messages or booking details.

The email option opens a draft in your configured email app with a suggested
subject and a link to the current page. The link excludes search parameters and
fragments. You compose, review, and send the message yourself; opening a draft
does not deliver an email. A plain email address is provided if no app opens.
The widget adds no custom analytics events or conversation storage; the site's
existing analytics and external-service policies still apply.

### Email correspondence

If you contact me by email, I receive the email address, message, and attachments
you send. I use them to respond to your enquiry and keep relevant correspondence.
Please do not send passwords, financial details, or other unnecessary sensitive
information.

I do not operate an on-site visitor-data sales service. The third-party
processing described above is governed by the providers' terms and settings.

## Retention, international processing, and your choices

Email correspondence is kept as needed to respond and maintain relevant records.
Analytics and hosting records are retained according to the applicable account
settings and provider policies; this notice does not promise a fixed deletion
period for provider-controlled records.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own.
Their privacy notices describe their processing and transfer arrangements.

You can control cookies through your browser settings and remove locally stored
preferences. Blocking cookies or third-party resources may affect functionality.
These browser controls do not replace consent requirements where they apply.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction,
or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain
processing. Contact [sajeefx@gmail.com](mailto:sajeefx@gmail.com) with your request.
I will respond as applicable to the information I control; information held by
external services may need to be addressed with those providers. You may also
have the right to contact your local data protection authority.
