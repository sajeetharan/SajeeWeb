# Contact

This personal website and blog is maintained by **Sajeetharan Sinnathurai**.
It shares my experience with developer tooling, Azure Cosmos DB, AI agents,
software engineering, and community work. Views expressed here are my own.

## Site enquiries and corrections

Email [sajeefx@gmail.com](mailto:sajeefx@gmail.com) for questions about articles,
projects, speaking, broken links, or factual corrections.

When reporting an issue with an article, include the page URL and explain what
needs attention. For an attribution or content-rights concern, identify the
specific material and provide relevant ownership or licensing details.

Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive details.
There is no guaranteed response time.
